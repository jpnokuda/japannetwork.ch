WAGNER Micro Ceramic Oil (WAGNER MC Oil)

Mr. Wagner consulting at a client's factory

"After 20 years distributing WAGNER products across diverse industry sectors in Japan, we can confidently affirm: these are truly once-in-a-lifetime technologies."

Micro Ceramic is an advanced solid ceramic lubricant made of ultra-fine particles, from 0.02 to 0.15 μm — the smallest in its class, with the lowest friction coefficient of any solid material. Blended with oil or applied independently, it improves energy efficiency, extends machine lifespan, and increases operational stability, while remaining chemically neutral toward standard oil compositions.

"Dramatically reducing friction and wear to unlock a machine's true performance and lifespan."

Mr. Wagner, a mechanical engineer, began his own research to solve the wear and failures caused by conventional lubricants. In cooperation with the University of Munich, this led to the artificial solid lubricant known as "Micro Ceramic technology." Today, WAGNER products are trusted worldwide, from classic cars and the latest high-performance vehicles to precision industrial machinery.

  • Improved fuel economy and lower CO₂ emissions
  • Reduced operating noise and vibration
