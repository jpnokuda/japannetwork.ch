WAGNER Micro Ceramic Oil (WAGNER MC Oil)

Over two decades of successful WAGNER product distribution in the Japanese market. We are now seeking a reliable distribution partner in Asia with an established network and relevant market expertise.

"After 15 years distributing WAGNER products across diverse industry sectors in Japan, we can confidently affirm: these are truly once-in-a-lifetime technologies."

Micro Ceramic is an advanced solid ceramic lubricant made of ultra-fine particles, from 0.02 to 0.15 μm — the smallest in its class, with the lowest friction coefficient of any solid material. Blended with oil or applied independently, it improves energy efficiency, extends machine lifespan, and increases operational stability, while remaining chemically neutral toward standard oil compositions.
