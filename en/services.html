Services

Trusted consulting and partnerships bridging Japan and Europe.

Based in Zurich, we support European companies entering the Japanese market as well as Japanese companies expanding into Europe. We provide comprehensive solutions ranging from market research to local partner search, contract negotiations, and sole distribution of specialty products.

1. Cross-Border Business Consulting

Our experts, deeply familiar with business customs and market structures in both Japan and Europe, support you from market research and local partner development to agency selection and contract negotiations.

2. Sole Importer of Specialty Lubricants

As the sole importer for Japan of high-performance Micro-Ceramic Lubricants (MC Oil) and additives from Germany's WAGNER, we build distribution networks and provide technical support.

3. Cross-Border Marketing & Communication

Overcoming language barriers (Japanese, German, English), we provide full support for branding, promotional planning, and Web/PR production tailored to local target audiences.
